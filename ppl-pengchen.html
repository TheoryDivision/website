I am a PhD student in Physics at Case Western Reserve University with a CAMPEP-accredited MS in Medical Physics from Purdue University, aspiring to become an academic-clinical medical physicist. My research focuses on developing quantitative methods to analyze tumor evolution and treatment response, particularly by assessing temporal changes in tumor radiomics and transcriptomics during radiation therapy and integrating them to enhance outcome prediction. Additionally, I explore theoretical approaches to studying fitness landscapes and optimizing therapeutic strategies. In my free time, I enjoy playing basketball, discovering new restaurants, and passionately following my Golden State Warriors.
